# 🌟 smarter.day — Smarter Planning, Better Living 🌟  

Welcome to **smarter.day**, the ultimate productivity app designed to help you organize, prioritize, and achieve your goals effortlessly. 

From task management to habit creation, smarter.day is here to transform how you plan and execute your daily life.

---

## 📖 About smarter.day  

smarter.day combines the latest science of productivity with an intuitive user experience to make every day your most productive yet. 

Here’s what smarter.day offers:  

### **Features**  

- [x] **"Eat That Frog" Methodology** 🐸: Start your day with your most important task.  
- [x] **Task Prioritization** 📝: Use the Eisenhower Matrix to stay focused on what matters most.
- [x] **Task Hardness** 💪: Define how hard this task is.
- [x] **Smart Score** 🧠: With special complex formula from task longevity, hardness and priority - calculate the **Smart Score**.
      See, what to do first and never waste time on unnecessary things.  
- [x] **Habit Building** 🌱: Create life-changing habits.  
- [x] **Team Collaboration** 🤝: Share projects and tasks with your team effortlessly.
- [x] **Smart Notifications** 🔔: Scientifically optimized reminders and nudges.
- [x] **Timeline View** 🗓️: Plan your day visually with roadmaps and time blocks.
- [x] **Backlog Space** 📂: A dedicated area to collect and organize ideas.
- [x] **Offline Mode** 📴: Stay productive even without internet access.  
- [ ] **Calendar Sync** 📅: Integration with Google and Apple Calendars.
- [ ] **Energy Management** ⚡: Align tasks with your energy levels for maximum efficiency.
- [ ] **Pomodoro Timers** ⏱️: Stay in the zone and avoid burnout.
- [ ] **AI Task Assistant** 🤖: Intelligent analysis to optimize task order and priorities.  
- [ ] **Advanced Analytics** 📊: Insights into your productivity trends.  
- [ ] **Focus Mode** 🎯: Reduce distractions with a minimalist interface.  
- [ ] **Advanced Sharing** 📤: Share tasks, projects, and habits with specific permissions.
